**Title (optional, for your reference):** The Tick Protocol: Time as Counted Distinction, Not Container Seconds

**Word limit:** 500 words or less

---

We keep time with clocks. Clocks measure duration between moments. They do poorly at a harder task: what happened, in what order, and can anyone prove it later?

In an age of infinite plausible assertion, duration is cheap and sequence is fragile. Archives rot. Models speak without committing to when anything occurred. We have strong protocols for the second. We have weak protocols for the tick: the indivisible mark that something occurred.

The Tick Protocol proposes marking time as a count of distinctions, not an invisible flow of seconds. A tick is one +1, one irreversible act of distinguishing before from after. Time is not a container we move through. Time is the accumulation of ticks. We count first. We measure duration second.

**Imagination.** Time becomes geometry you can teach at any scale. A kindergartener learns: time is dots we count. A watchmaker learns: cycles wrap. A philosopher asks: what counts as one tick in a society? Seven panels trace the spine: dot, line, mod-N circle, phase wedge, helix, deep-time coil, spiral pitch. Ordinal time precedes metric time. Pi appears as the geometry of return, not decoration.

**Behavior.** The protocol schedules silence. Eight three-hour prahars structure a day, plus Amrit Vela (prahar 7): no new ticks, only settlement. Decay without new input. A designed pause, not an accident. An hourglass visualization places a Landauer floor at the waist where a system flips between accumulation and commitment. You feel phase, not just timestamp.

**Decision-making.** Three marking rules create a civic ledger. Rule 1: atomic tick, one act, one cost (action A in the efficiency law E = ΔI/A). Rule 2: append-only sequence; reordering is detectable via HelixHash chains that commit each entry to index, payload, and prior hash. Rule 3: external anchor (for example RFC 3161 timestamping) so self-asserted time cannot be quietly rewritten. Five-digit years (02026) situate marks in civilizational time. Decisions gain a verifiable before and after.

**Prototype (eight weeks if selected).** HelixClock CLI: append tick, verify chain, export head hash with 02026 anchor. Phase dial: hourglass position from live or demo state. Prahar ring: eight segments, segment 7 locked to settlement-only. Public demo: one day, N ticks, one verifiable export. HelixHash exists. The geometry exists. The gap is packaging for civic use.

The 10,000-Year Clock measures long duration in matter. The Tick Protocol preserves long sequence in evidence. Clocks and chains are complements, not rivals.

Time as dot. Line. Circle. Helix. Chain.
