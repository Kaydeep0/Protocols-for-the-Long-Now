## Figure 1. The dot (+1)

**File:** `figures/01_dot_plus_one.png`  
**Caption:** The atomic tick. One distinction creates before and after. The first mark of time is not a second. It is a +1: one bit, one act, one cost. Binary decision structure shows that distinction is discrete before it is continuous.

---

## Figure 2. Tick count (time as line)

**File:** `figures/02_tick_count_line.png`  
**Caption:** Repeated +1 gives time as tick count. The stem plot x[n] shows discrete moments on a line. Time is ordinal before it is metric. We count distinctions first. Duration is derived later.

---

## Figure 3. Cycle (mod N, the circle)

**File:** `figures/03_cycle_mod_n_circle.jpg`  
**Caption:** Finite budget makes cycles. When counting wraps (n mod N), the line closes into a circle. Residue classes show that 0, 7, 14, and so on occupy the same phase slot. Pi enters as geometry of the cycle, not as mysticism.

---

## Figure 4. Phase on the circle (pi as wedge)

**File:** `figures/04_phase_circle_pi.png`  
**Caption:** The dot on a cycle acquires angular position and spread. The wedge delta-theta is the "pie slice" of phase uncertainty. Concentric circles are energy levels. Same cycle, different amplitude.

---

## Figure 5. Helix (CAD: dots become solid cycle)

**File:** `figures/05_helix_cad.png`  
**Caption:** Primitive geometry: dot to circle to helix. Wireframe ticks along a helical path become solid plates. Each rotation is a cycle. Vertical advance is accumulation. Time as geometry you can build.

---

## Figure 6. Helix through time (past positions)

**File:** `figures/06_helix_through_time.png`  
**Caption:** Cycles embedded through time. Past data sits on earlier coils. The observable present sits on a later coil. Same structural return, different level. Deep time as a path, not a label.

---

## Figure 7. Spiral pitch (Fibonacci-like scale)

**File:** `figures/07_spiral_pitch_fibonacci.png`  
**Caption:** Spiral pitch and expanding radius between cycles. Each turn is wider and higher than the last. Memory across cycles (Fibonacci recurrence in the full protocol) prevents coherence loss at the narrow waist between accumulation and commitment.
